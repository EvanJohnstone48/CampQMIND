# Lane 4 · World View & God Console

You build what everyone sees: the 3D valley in the browser and the god's control panel. The valley should feel alive: little bean miners walking trails to the mines, farms and forest, with mountains, a river and buildings, all made from simple shapes in code (no 3D modelling). The console is where the god moves dials, uses powers, and watches charts. Make it look good and feel powerful, because that's what people remember from the demo.

**Your folders:** `apps/web` (layout, `net`, `ui`, `features/world`, `features/economy`, `features/console`)

**Things you'll make:**
- The page layout: top bar, left panel, tabs in the middle, narrator on the right, timeline at the bottom
- A live connection to the server, and a shared store all panels read from
- 3D terrain, river, trails, buildings and labels
- Beans that walk to their jobs; click one to select it
- Sliders for every dial, buttons for god powers and acts of god
- Charts of the economy over time
- Effects (lightning, gold shower), day/night, and a timeline you can undo from
